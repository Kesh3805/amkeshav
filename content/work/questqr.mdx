## What it does

Businesses run dynamic QR campaigns with sponsor ads, live scanning, redemption tracking and dashboards. The scan path is where correctness matters most.

<Flow steps={["Scan", "Pre-validation", "Inventory check", "Redis lock", "Atomic redemption", "SSE stats → dashboard"]} />

## What I built

- The scan path: pre-checks before the database to shed load, then an atomic inventory decrement under a Redis lock so concurrent scans can't double-redeem.
- The campaign lifecycle engine — draft → live → completed → inactive — with timezone-aware validation, so campaigns activate and expire correctly across regions.
- Sponsor ads with a server-side UTM redirect whose parameters are prebuilt, so the redirect itself does no lookup.
- Live inventory stats over SSE; QR generation with logo embedding and Japanese text on generated PDFs.

## Decisions

- Archived campaign lines are filtered in the query, not the service, so deleted data is never instantiated as an entity.
- Ad placement moved from a strategy pattern to direct injection into the response pipeline — less indirection for one real variation.
