## What it does

Two independent approaches, because a single model signature is fragile. Fine-tuned ViT/DeiT and RoBERTa/DeBERTa classifiers look for generative artifacts; Error Level Analysis, EXIF anomalies and stylometry look at physical and statistical signals that don't depend on having seen a particular generator.

## Decisions

- **Explanations are output, not decoration.** Grad-CAM heatmaps for images, SHAP per sentence for text.
- **Built for evidence.** The PDF report carries a SHA-256 of the original artifact. Datasets are versioned with DVC so retraining is reproducible.
