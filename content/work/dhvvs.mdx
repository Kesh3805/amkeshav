## What it does

Community health workers report visits on paper or simple apps, which invites ghost visits — and ordinary databases allow retroactive edits. DHVVS makes each visit a chain of evidence.

<Flow steps={["NFC / encrypted QR + geofence", "ECDSA sign on device", "NestJS ingestion", "BullMQ", "Fraud scoring · L2 anchor", "Patient OTP confirm"]} />

## Decisions

- **Sign before the network.** The backend verifies a claim instead of creating one.
- **Keep the slow parts async.** Fraud scoring (impossible travel, overlapping windows, stationary GPS clusters) and anchoring are queued, off the ingestion path.
- **Anchor a hash, not the record.** The chain gets a hash of the verified visit; the relational record stays in Postgres.

Four apps in a Turborepo: an offline-first Expo mobile app, the NestJS backend, an admin dashboard with geospatial heatmaps, and a minimal patient portal.
