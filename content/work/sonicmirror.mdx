## What it does

OAuth PKCE with no stored credentials, top artists across three time windows, per-track audio features aggregated into a normalised fingerprint, then a three-stage Gemini chain — archetypes, tendencies, calibrated humour — streamed token by token.

## The one decision

The prompts forbid the generic answer and force specific, falsifiable claims tied to the numbers. Getting a language model to be *specific* was the whole project.
