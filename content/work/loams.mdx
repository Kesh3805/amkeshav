## What it does

The first line of the README is the whole premise: don't rent a cloud. Loams keeps data in the user's own object-storage bucket, in open formats, and the engine speaks existing wire protocols — so a team can point a Qdrant, Elasticsearch, Postgres or MySQL client at it instead of adopting a new API.

Around that sits tooling for building software with AI agents: an agent daemon, durable execution, a console, a desktop app, a CLI and SDKs.

## Why it exists

Most data platforms that serve AI workloads ask you to move your data into their account and learn their API. Loams bets on the opposite: your bucket, open formats, protocols you already have clients for. It's Apache-2.0 with no open core and no paid edition.

## How it's built

A Rust monorepo of roughly eighty crates, grouped by concern — the engine (query, collections, an HNSW index, text search, the log and the store), a compatibility layer per wire protocol, metadata and storage, durable execution, the graph, and the agent daemon. It builds on established components rather than reinventing them: DataFusion for query, Lance for columnar storage, Tantivy for text, SlateDB, openraft and TiKV among others.

## My part

I'm one of two listed maintainers in a flat governance structure. I'm deliberately not claiming authorship of specific subsystems here: the public history doesn't attribute individual components, and the design decisions belong to the project.

## Evidence, as the project publishes it

Loams makes no headline benchmark claims. What it publishes is more useful: raw write-path and commit benchmarks comparing NVMe write strategies — p50/p99 latency, throughput, CPU per transaction, noise bands over repeated runs — including gate reports where configurations **fail** their target. Roadmap targets are stated as gates, not as results.

## Where it stands

Version 0.1.2, a patch release on the first release line, shipped on 26 October 2026. The project says plainly that APIs and on-disk formats may still change between minor versions.
