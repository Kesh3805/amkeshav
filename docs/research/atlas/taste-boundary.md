# Taste boundary

ChatGPT asked how much visual experimentation a site can sustain before Keshav stops finding it sophisticated. Keshav watched eight real-speed recordings in the [Taste Boundary study](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Bz62UuETpdzibmNDK4oTF), marked moments and gave verdicts (1 Nov 2026). The raw marks are in `study/marks.json`. `study/marks-sheet.jpg` shows the frame at each mark, and `study/marks-context.jpg` shows two seconds either side.

**How strong this evidence is.** One pass of about two minutes, one mark per clip, no written notes, and verdicts on five of eight clips. The marks are precise about *when*, but *why* is our reading of the frames. Treat the principles below as strong hypotheses, not settled rules.

## What changed between stills and motion

Two verdicts flipped, and they are the most informative result:
- **Rauno went from loved to "too playful".** As a still it reads as Swiss restraint. In motion, big coloured discs sliding sideways read as toy-like.
- **OXMAN went from not chosen to "sophisticated".** As a still it is a quiet white page. In motion it is real footage of a real lab, framed by one hairline circle.

So stills overrated colour-led playfulness and underrated quiet, documentary authority. The 12-site board alone would have pointed the wrong way on both.

## Principles (hypotheses, strongly suggested)

**Positive**
1. **Form that assembles itself is the extraordinary moment.** Three of his marks land on a construction rather than on a finished state: the clocks redrawing, the wordmark wiping in, the relief growing. The motion he responds to *builds* the composition. It does not decorate it or bounce it around.
2. **Arrival without waiting.** Making Software was marked at 2.2 s, the earliest mark in the study, for a page that is simply already there.
3. **Documentary authority reads as sophisticated.** OXMAN's real people and real material, presented quietly, won him over in motion.
4. **One graphic system, held strictly.** Every positive mark sits inside a strict system: drawing language, clock grid, wordmark, plaster, ink.

**Negative**
1. **Colour-led playfulness in motion.** Saturated discs and sliding cards (Rauno) tip over, even when the still looks restrained.
2. **Product drama and wit as personality** (Teenage Engineering) reads as "too playful", even though nothing on screen is cartoonish.
3. **Spectacle intros are tolerated, not loved.** Nothin's black intro with an inflatable O earned only "Mixed". The mark went to the moment the intro *ended* and the giant wordmark took over.

**The boundary, in one sentence:** experimentation stays sophisticated while it is *constructive* (something being built, drawn, revealed or documented inside a strict monochrome-leaning system) and stops when it becomes *performative* (colour, characters, jokes or objects performing for the visitor).

This refines ChatGPT's "controlled visual disruption". The disruption he likes is a construction, not a surprise.

## ChatGPT's refinement (accepted)

ChatGPT accepted "constructive vs performative" as the leading hypothesis and challenged it with three counterexamples:
- Making Software was marked at 2.2 s, before anything moved.
- OXMAN builds nothing.
- The Odd Works mark is on a static logotype.

So Keshav doesn't specifically want things to assemble themselves. He values **visual authorship becoming visible**. The working principle is now:

> **Strong graphic authorship first. Motion should reveal, construct or substantiate the visual idea, not compete with it.**

Two cautions from ChatGPT that we keep:
- "Performative" does not mean colourful, and "constructive" does not mean monochrome.
- Don't turn this into a ban on cinematic ambition. Every extraordinary moment should feel intentional; nothing needs to be quieter for its own sake.

## Rauno, settled

Asked what bothered him most when Rauno turned "too playful", Keshav chose **all three**: the bright colours, the sideways movement, and the feeling that the motion was performing rather than creating or revealing anything. The boundary is therefore both formal (saturated colour as the lead) and behavioural (motion grammar and purpose). One of these alone would not have been enough to explain the flip.

**Confidence:** moderate. The evidence is precise in time but small in quantity.

## Still unknown

- Verdicts on Making Software, Immersive Garden and Odd Works were left blank. Their marks were positive, but "loved, no reservations" is not confirmed.
- Whether the Teenage Engineering objection is to wit, to the dark spotlight register, or to the dense navigation.
