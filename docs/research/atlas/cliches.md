# Clichés and techniques to avoid

Taken from the 48 GPU captures in this folder (1 Nov 2026), not from opinion alone. Each entry names the sites where we saw it, so the judgement can be checked against the sheets.

## First frame

- **The percentage loader.** Seen at Lusion (000 → 079), Fabian Tjoe-A-On (stuck at 069), Mathis Biabiany (stuck at 17), itomdev (60% → 90%), Daiki Fujita (087 → 090), Sea We Breathe (dial). A number counting up is the most common first impression among award sites. It promises spectacle and delays it. Two sites froze on it for more than a minute on real hardware.
- **Black screen, then a wordmark.** Seen at Active Theory, Junni, Nature Beyond Tech, OceanX and Symphony of Vines. The first four seconds show nothing.
- **"Best with headphones" / sound gates.** Seen at Voyage Azarien, Sea We Breathe and Unseen. A gate before content is only earned when the gate is itself part of the fiction (Henry Heffernan's BIOS boot, Shader's CRT boot).
- **"Click to enter" with nothing to see behind it.** Seen at Daiki Fujita, OceanX and Messenger. The visitor commits before they know why.

## Art direction

- **The dark glass and particles look.** Seen at Active Theory, Nature Beyond Tech and Phantom: iridescent glass ring, drifting particles, teal-black gradient. It reads as "WebGL studio" and nothing more.
- **Cyberpunk neon city.** Seen at Samsy: magenta and cyan signs, red HUD. Technically impressive, visually borrowed.
- **The spinning Earth hero.** Seen at OceanX, Paper Planes and Radio Garden. It works only when the globe *is* the interface (Radio Garden), not as a backdrop for a headline (OceanX).
- **Surreal pastel architecture.** Seen at Unseen: arches, water and a sphere in pink. It has been everywhere since 2020.
- **The HUD overlay.** Seen at Nature Beyond Tech ("SCANNING", crosshairs) and Samsy. Sci-fi chrome signalling "technology" without saying anything.
- **Logo as the 3D finale.** Seen at Igloo (the scroll ends in a sculpted logo ring) and Active Theory. The journey resolves into branding.

## Layout and copy

- **"Play Reel".** Seen at Lusion and Exo Ape. The same full-bleed reel block, with the same words, on agency after agency.
- **"Creating the unexpected / Bold ideas, brought to life."** Seen at Unseen and Lusion. Interchangeable positioning lines set huge.
- **"CREATIVE DEVELOPER" as the headline.** Seen at Wodniack. Strong execution, but the label is the most generic thing on the page.
- **Award badges as the content.** Seen at Wodniack (an awards wall) and Nothin' (Site of the Day ribbon). It proves taste to juries, not ability to visitors.
- **Tech-logo stickers.** Seen at itomdev (JS, Node, TS badges on the door). It is the skills-bar of 2026.

## Interaction

- **Scroll hijack that just plays a film.** Seen at Igloo, Active Theory and Oryzo. Gorgeous, but the visitor only controls playback speed. Fine for a product; for a person it says nothing about how they think.
- **A modal that steals the page.** Seen at Shopify, where a video modal took over after one click and every later frame was the same video.
- **Cookie banners that only offer "Accept" visibly.** Seen at Phantom, Symphony of Vines and Ponpon Mania. Small, but cheap-feeling on otherwise premium pages.

## What the clean, premium sites avoid

The calm, finished sites (Immersive Garden, Making Software, Rauno, Kenta Toshikura, Nothin', d/dt, Exo Ape, Lando Norris) do three things the others don't:
1. **The first frame is already the design.** It is type and one image, with no loader.
2. **One material, held all the way through**: plaster relief, blueprint linework, black-and-white portal circles, paper.
3. **Motion responds to the visitor**, through hover reveals, a helmet wiping away or a cursor sculpting the wordmark, rather than playing at them.
